"I (or my parent/spouse) am having trouble hearing. I want someone nearby in Victoria I can trust to check my hearing, tell me honestly whether I need hearing aids, help me choose them, and keep helping afterwards — without surprise costs."

### 3.2 Likely audience segments `[INFERRED]`

### 3.3 What the visitor expects after the click
1. **Confirmation it's local** — Victoria address, map, phone.
2. **What happens first** — hearing test/consultation, how long it takes (up to ~1 hour plus discussion, S7).
3. **Cost reassurance** — is the first visit free? Are there hidden fees? Financing?
4. **Who they will see** — a real local specialist, not a call center.
5. **Proof** — Google rating and local reviews.
6. **Easy next step** — short form or one-tap call.
7. **Unanswerable-today questions** — insurance/Medicare, exact prices, trial/returns (A#3, A#13). The page should route these to a call rather than make claims.
