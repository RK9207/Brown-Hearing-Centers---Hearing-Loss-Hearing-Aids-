From understanding your hearing to ongoing support

Assessment comes first. Hearing aids enter the conversation only if they’re right for you.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Understand your hearing

    A hearing evaluation is a series of tests, including how well you detect and understand speech, so you know where things stand. Plan for up to an hour plus time to talk through the results. Bring a family member if you’d like. Hearing loss affects the people around you too.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Talk through options that fit you

    If hearing aids may help, your specialist explains which sounds are hardest for you and how devices could help. Options are matched to your hearing, lifestyle and budget, and the final choice is yours.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Get fitted and learn to use them

    Your hearing aids are programmed to your hearing prescription. You practice putting them in and taking them out, charging or changing batteries, and cleaning them. Fitting appointments generally take at least an hour, and written instructions are available on request.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Adjust and stay supported

    Getting used to new sounds takes time, and fine-tuning can take a few appointments. Regular cleanings and check-ins continue after that. Lifetime device programming is among the complimentary services Brown lists, so ask what’s included.

Every visit is different. Timing varies, and some people need more than one adjustment appointment.

Step one can be a free consultation. Request an appointment and we’ll take it from there.

Hearing aid options that fit the way you live

Hearing aids aren’t one-size-fits-all. Your specialist helps you weigh options against your hearing, your routine and your budget, then teaches you to use them with confidence.

IMG-PRODUCT-01 · 4:3 landscape · Selective product visual (Signia or Starkey render, labeled as an example)

Chosen for your hearing, lifestyle and budget

Your specialist considers how much you hear, what your days look like (church, family meals, hobbies, work) and what you want to spend. You can compare options that make sense for you, and the choice stays yours. Not everyone needs hearing aids, which is why testing comes first.

Features that can make everyday use simpler

Depending on the model, hearing aids may be rechargeable, connect by Bluetooth to a compatible phone for calls and streaming, or come in discreet styles that sit mostly out of sight. Brown carries Signia and Starkey hearing aids. Features, styles and phone compatibility vary by model, and the smallest styles suit mild to moderate hearing loss.

Hands-on help learning your hearing aids

Your fitting includes learning to put them in and take them out, charge or change batteries, and keep them clean. Settings can be fine-tuned at follow-up visits as you get used to new sounds. That practice and follow-up can help new hearing aids feel more natural over time.

Cost matters, so we talk about it early

Our pricing is all-inclusive, and we’re happy to walk through exactly what that covers for the hearing aids you’re considering. Financing is available through CareCredit and Cherry, subject to each provider’s terms and approval.

What else would you like to know before you reach out?

Is the consultation free?

Yes, you can start with a free consultation. Brown’s complimentary services include one-on-one consultations, otoscopic examinations and hearing screenings. A complete hearing evaluation is a separate service, so ask us what your first visit will include and whether any costs apply.

What happens at a hearing evaluation, and can someone come with me?

A hearing evaluation is a series of tests, including how well you detect and understand speech. Your specialist explains the results and, if hearing aids may help, goes over your options. Plan on up to an hour plus time to talk. Bringing a family member is encouraged, because hearing loss affects the people around you too.

How much do hearing aids cost, and are payment options available?

Cost depends mainly on the technology level and features you choose, not on the style. Prices aren’t listed here because the right option depends on your hearing, lifestyle and budget. Our pricing is all-inclusive, so ask us what it covers. CareCredit and Cherry financing are available, subject to approval. Have a coverage question? Ask us when you call or request an appointment.

How do I know which hearing aid style is right for me?

The right style depends on your type and degree of hearing loss, dexterity and vision, ear anatomy, lifestyle, appearance preferences and budget. Some of the smallest styles suit mild to moderate hearing loss, while others work across a wider range. Your specialist will explain what fits, and why.

Will I get help learning to use and adjust to my hearing aids?

Yes. At your fitting, your specialist programs the devices and shows you how to put them in, take them out, charge or change batteries and clean them. Adjusting to new sounds takes time and may take a few follow-up appointments. Written instructions are available on request, and regular cleanings and check-ins are part of ongoing care.

Can you help with hearing aids I bought somewhere else?

Brown repairs and services hearing aids from most major manufacturers, including some it didn’t fit. Contact us with the brand and what’s going wrong, and we’ll tell you what can be done. Out-of-warranty repairs may involve a small fee, and we can’t promise every device is repairable.
